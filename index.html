About me

Warm, evidence-based care

I'm a licensed therapist with over a decade of experience helping adults and young people find their footing through difficult seasons. My approach blends cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT), mindfulness, and somatic techniques tailored to each individual.

I believe therapy is most effective when it feels like a true partnership — one built on trust, curiosity, and genuine care. You bring your story; I bring the tools and space for it to unfold.
